# 🌦️🎵 WeatherTunes

> The weather outside becomes your personal DJ.  
> Instantly get the perfect playlist for rain, sunshine, snow, or fog.

![screenshot](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/placeholder/screenshot.png) <!-- add your own -->

## ✨ What It Does

WeatherTunes grabs your **current location** (with your permission) and checks the weather using the **free Open‑Meteo API**. It then translates the weather condition into a **vibe** (e.g., “Rainy Lo‑Fi”, “Sunny Indie Pop”) and provides one‑click buttons to open **Spotify** or **YouTube** directly with that exact search.

No accounts, no keys, no backend – everything runs in your browser.

## 🎯 Features

- 📍 Geolocation – weather for *your* spot (or fallback to San Francisco)
- ☀️🌧️❄️ 21 weather codes mapped to hand‑curated vibes
- 🎧 Spotify & YouTube deep links – open and hit play
- 🎨 Dynamic background gradients that match the weather
- ⚡ Instant, privacy‑first – no data leaves your device
- 📱 Responsive, glass‑morphism UI

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your favourite browser.
3. Allow location access when prompted.
4. Let the weather decide your soundtrack.
